Solution

Introducing Soteria.
Intelligent protection against vishing.

An AI-powered cybersecurity app that helps organizations protect employees from voice phishing. It analyses phone conversations in real time to spot suspicious language, social-engineering tactics and attempts to extract confidential information.

TRADITIONAL TOOLS

Check the phone number

Spam lists only catch known numbers. A new number or a spoofed one gets straight through.

SOTERIA

Understand the conversation

Flags threats based on what the caller says and how they try to manipulate the employee.

How it works

The Soteria process

01

TRANSCRIBE

Turns the caller's speech into text on the device, as they speak.

02

ANALYSE

Google Gemini checks every sentence for impersonation, urgency, secrecy and requests for codes, links or payment. Each check takes about 1 second.

03

ASSESS

Scores the risk from 0 to 100 and lists the warning signs it found, using a sensitivity the company sets.

04

ALERT

Shows a red banner, vibrates and sends a notification with what to do next. Every call is logged for review.

OUR DIFFERENTIATOR
Traditional security identifies suspicious callers.
Soteria identifies suspicious conversations.
